# Fault tickets
ref: 3.1
also: internet wifi data network signal coverage speed slow dropping drops dropout dropouts cutting out keeps disconnecting broken not working reception outage fault ticket

A reported service fault — dropouts, no service, degraded speed — requires a **fault ticket raised
on the call**, with the ticket number read out to the customer.

Target response times from the ticket being raised:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| total loss of service | 4 business hours |
| intermittent fault | 2 business days |
| degraded performance | 5 business days |

Telling a customer their fault has been "logged" or "noted" without a ticket number is not
compliance with this policy. If the same fault is reported a second time, the ticket must be
escalated to the network team rather than a second ticket raised.
