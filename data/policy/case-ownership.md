# Case ownership
ref: 2.2
also: who owner owns responsible name handling dealing looking after accountable queue passed nobody

Every case that cannot be closed on the first contact must have a **named owner** before the call
ends, and the customer must be told that name.

Placing a case in a team queue does not satisfy this. A queue has no accountable individual, so
nobody is answerable for the deadline, and in practice the case is picked up by whoever is next
free — which is why these cases are the ones that come back.

If the agent cannot assign an owner themselves, they must transfer to a team leader before ending
the call rather than promising a follow-up they cannot guarantee.
