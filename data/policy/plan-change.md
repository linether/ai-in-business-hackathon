# Plan changes
ref: 8.1
also: plan package upgrade downgrade change plan wrong plan tier data allowance

Plan changes take effect from the **next billing period** unless the customer is moving to a higher
plan, which can take effect immediately on request.

Where a customer was placed on the wrong plan by Meridian Mobile, the change is backdated to the
date of the error and the difference refunded. The customer is not required to have noticed the
error within any particular period.
