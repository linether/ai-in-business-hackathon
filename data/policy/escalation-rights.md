# Escalation on request
ref: 5.2
also: supervisor manager team leader escalate complaint speak to someone higher

A customer who asks for a supervisor, a manager or a complaint to be raised must be given one. This
is not subject to the agent's assessment of whether it is warranted.

If no team leader is available, the agent takes the customer's details and a team leader calls back
within **4 business hours** — a commitment which, like any other, requires a named owner under
policy 2.1.
