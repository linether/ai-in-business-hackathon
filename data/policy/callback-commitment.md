# Callback commitment
ref: 2.1
also: callback call back ring ringing promised promise nobody called never called waiting heard nothing follow up chase

When an agent tells a customer someone will call them back, the callback must happen within
**24 hours** of the commitment, during business hours.

The commitment is only valid once it has a **named owner** recorded on the case. "I will pass this
to the billing team", "it goes into the queue" and "someone will look at it" are not commitments
under this policy, because no individual is accountable for them and the 24-hour clock cannot be
enforced against a queue.

If the callback cannot be made within 24 hours, the agent who made the commitment must contact the
customer before the deadline expires and give a new date. A silent overrun is treated as a failure
of the commitment regardless of what happens afterwards.
