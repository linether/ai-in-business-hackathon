# Total loss of service
ref: 3.2
also: no service cut off disconnected suspended nothing works cannot call can't call dead line no signal outage

A customer with no working service is treated as urgent regardless of the underlying cause,
including where the cause is a billing suspension that is itself in dispute.

Service must be restored, or an interim arrangement offered, **before** the billing question is
resolved. An agent must never leave a customer without service pending an investigation.

Where service has been lost for more than 24 hours the customer is entitled to a pro-rata credit,
which the agent applies without the customer having to ask.
