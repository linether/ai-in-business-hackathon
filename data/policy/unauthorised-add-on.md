# Unauthorised add-ons
ref: 4.2
also: add-on addon subscription extra charge charged didn't sign up never agreed never authorised unauthorised money back refund reverse bundle

Where a customer states they did not authorise a recurring add-on, the agent must:

1. **Remove the add-on immediately**, on the call. This is not subject to investigation and does not
   require a supervisor.
2. Refund **every charge for that add-on**, with no limit on how many billing periods back, where
   the customer says they did not authorise it and no record of consent exists.
3. Record the removal and the refund on the case before ending the call.

Consent is evidenced by a recorded authorisation or a written confirmation. Absence of evidence is
resolved **in the customer's favour** — the burden is on Meridian Mobile to show consent was given,
not on the customer to show it was not.
