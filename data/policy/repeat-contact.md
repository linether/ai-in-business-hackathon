# Repeat contact
ref: 5.1
also: again third time second time keep calling rang before called before same problem every time

The **third** contact about the same unresolved matter must be escalated to a team leader before the
call ends. The agent does not need the customer to ask.

Second contacts about a matter that is still outstanding must be flagged on the case so the pattern
is visible. Repeat contact is the strongest predictor we have of a complaint reaching the Ombudsman,
and the cost of escalating early is far lower than the cost of a referral.
