# Contradictory answers
ref: 6.1
also: told me different last person said other agent said contradicts changed story different answer

Where a customer reports that a previous agent told them something different, the agent must check
the case history **before** responding, and must not restate the current position as though the
earlier one was never given.

If the earlier answer was wrong, say so plainly and honour the position most favourable to the
customer where both were within policy. Contradictory answers across contacts are treated as a
handling failure attributable to Meridian Mobile, not to the customer's misunderstanding.
