# Retention and churn intent
ref: 7.2
also: cancel cancelling leave leaving switch switching port move moving another provider competitor quit contract end terminate retention

Where a customer says they intend to leave, port their number, or not renew, the agent must not
route them to retention until the original matter is resolved or assigned an owner.

Offering a discount in place of resolving the complaint is prohibited. It converts a handling
failure into a pricing conversation, the underlying matter stays open, and the customer contacts us
again — usually to the Ombudsman.
