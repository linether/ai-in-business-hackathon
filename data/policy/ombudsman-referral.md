# Ombudsman referral
ref: 7.1
also: ombudsman tio regulator formal complaint report you authority acma legal

If a customer mentions the Telecommunications Industry Ombudsman, the TIO, or says they intend to
make a formal complaint, the agent must:

1. Escalate to a team leader before the call ends.
2. Record the mention on the case verbatim.
3. Not attempt to talk the customer out of it, and not suggest it will delay their resolution.

Meridian Mobile has **10 business days** to resolve a matter referred by the TIO before it becomes a
formal investigation, which carries a per-case fee regardless of the outcome. A matter that comes
back to the Ombudsman after referral is the most expensive outcome available to us, which is why
early escalation is cheaper than being right.
