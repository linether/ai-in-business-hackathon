# Refund window
ref: 4.1
also: refund money back credit reimburse overcharged charge billing bill dispute wrong amount

Billing disputes other than unauthorised add-ons are refundable for **two billing periods** from the
date the disputed charge appeared.

Outside that window a refund requires a team leader. The two-period limit does **not** apply where
the delay was caused by Meridian Mobile — for example where the customer raised the dispute inside
the window and it was not actioned. In that case the original contact date governs.

Agents must not quote the two-period limit to a customer who has already raised the matter once.
Doing so is a contradictory answer under policy 6.1.
