## The four tracks

You pick one of Tracks 1–3. **The ElevenLabs special track can be entered *alongside* it** — a second
shot at a $100 track prize for no extra submission.

### Track 1 — Improve an Existing Business Capability
Take something a business already does and make it significantly better with AI: faster, cheaper, more
accurate, more scalable, easier to use. Augmenting an existing capability, not inventing a new one.
Their examples: end-to-end support agent, document-processing pipeline for invoices/contracts, AI
coding or QA system, lead-qualifying sales assistant, multimodal inventory management, internal
knowledge agent that can take actions, better demand forecasting or scheduling.
> "Businesses already do X. How can AI make X dramatically better?"

### Track 2 — Create a New Business Capability
Build something a business previously *could not realistically do*. Agents, multimodal, voice, vision,
autonomous workflows, new human–AI interaction. Unlocking new possibilities, not optimising old ones.
Their examples: agent that autonomously negotiates with suppliers, voice system for dynamically
personalised products, continuously-running market research team, unstructured company data turned
into interactive simulations, autonomous service acting on behalf of customers.
> "What can a business do now that simply wasn't possible before?"

### Track 3 — Solve a Business Problem
Start from a concrete problem, not from a technology. Operational, financial, strategic, customer-facing
or industry-specific. Their examples: food/inventory waste, fraud detection, cash-flow understanding for
small business, scheduling conflicts, supply-chain risk, navigating complex regulation, supplier
matching, customer churn, accessibility of business services.
> "What is a real problem worth solving, and what can we build in 48 hours to attack it?"

### Special Track — Built With ElevenLabs
Open to projects from any of the three main tracks. ElevenLabs must be **core functionality, not a
bolted-on extra**. Their examples: real-time conversational voice agent, spoken sales/support agent,
multilingual voice interface, fully voice-driven workflow, interactive training/onboarding with dynamic
voices, personalised audio at scale.
> "What becomes possible when AI can naturally speak, listen, and interact through voice?"

## Submission requirements — Devpost, due Mon 12:00pm

1. **Track name** stated clearly in the project description.
2. **Public codebase** — must be a public repo accessible to judges. (Ours is private right now.)
3. **Production URL** — a live deployed app. *"Projects only running on localhost will be scored lower
   than a live, hosted deployment."* Not mandatory per the FAQ, but it costs real points.
4. **3–5 minute video** walkthrough showing the app working **end-to-end** — *"not just slides or
   narration over static screens."*

Finals submission (finalists only) due 5:30pm Mon; they get contacted with details.

## Final pitch (top 8 only)

3–5 minute **live** demo + verbal pitch covering problem, solution, value proposition. Live
demonstration of the working product — **a pre-recorded video is not accepted** at this stage.

## Rules

- Teams of **2–5**, at least one current university student (any institution).
- Teams must attend in person for finalists to receive prizes. You are *not* required to stay on campus
  during the event.
- **No restrictions on tools, APIs, or models.** Anything goes.
- Late submissions are not considered.
