# Event brief

Everything we know from the Luma page (https://luma.com/qmvfa2nk) as of 10 Sep 2026.
Update this file after opening night with anything the organisers say on stage.

## What it is

> "Forward is a 48-hour hackathon focused specifically on applying AI in a business setting."

Presented by Responsible AI Development (RAID) and DSCubed, hosted by Nathan Luo and RAID UniMelb at
the University of Melbourne, Parkville VIC 3010. Sponsored by **Eleno**, a Melbourne software firm
doing AI and automation work across several industries.

## Schedule

- **Opening night — Thu 10 Sep, 6:00–7:30pm**, Elisabeth Murdoch Building, G06 Theatre.
  Keynote from Eleno + team formation.
- **Hack starts — Sat 12 Sep, 12:00pm.** The three challenge tracks are revealed here, not before.
- **Submissions close — Mon 14 Sep, 12:00pm.**
- **Finalists announced — Mon 14 Sep, 4:00pm.**
- **Closing night — Mon 14 Sep, 5:30–8:30pm.** Finalist pitches and awards.

## Eligibility

- 2–5 people per team.
- At least one current university student on the team (any institution).
- In-person attendance required to be eligible as a finalist.
- Free entry, registration approved by the host.

## Prizes

| Place | Prize |
| --- | --- |
| 1st | $2,000 |
| 2nd | $500 |
| 3rd | $200 |
| Track winner (×3) | $100 |

ElevenLabs is giving every participant a 1-month Creator tier; winners get Pro/Scale tier access.
Worth assuming a **voice / audio angle scores well** with at least one sponsor.

Also on offer: mentors throughout the weekend, judges from academia and industry, coworking space,
and internship/job opportunities at Eleno.
